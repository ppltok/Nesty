Findings & fixes

1. Half of onboarding completers never add an item - and it regressed

What went wrong

Onboarding can be "completed" without adding a single product. This week 16 of 33 completers (48.5%) have an empty registry, up from 10 of 35 (28.6%) last week. The final onboarding step ("הרגע הכי כיף - יש לך קישור מחנות?") is skippable, and half the people skip it and then never return to add anything.

Evidence

Supabase: onboarding_completed=true AND 0 items = 48.5% (7d) vs 28.6% (prior 7d). Clarity: /onboarding dead-click on 50% of sessions suggests the add-item interaction on step 7 may not be obviously tappable on mobile.

Suggested fix

Make the empty state work for you. (a) On step 7, if the user skips, don't drop them on an empty dashboard - route to a pre-filled starter checklist ("add these 5 in one tap"). (b) Trigger the existing checklist_nudge email within hours (not days) for completers with 0 items. (c) Instrument step 7 in Clarity with a custom tag so we can see skip-vs-add directly. Start in nesty-web/src/pages/onboarding/Onboarding.tsx (step 7 block, ~line 1216) and the dashboard empty state.

2. Organic signups abandon onboarding at the very first screen

What went wrong

7 of 9 abandoners dropped at step 1 (first name) or before it ever recorded a step. These are all direct/organic - not ad traffic. The first screen ("בואי נכיר!") asks for a name immediately after signup; on mobile in-app browsers (IG/FB) that extra form field right after account creation is friction.

Evidence

Abandoner onboarding_last_step: null ×4, step 1 ×3, step 5 ×1, step 7 ×1. Clarity dead-click 50% on /onboarding, 88% mobile.

Suggested fix

Pre-fill first name from the auth profile (Google/email display name) so step 1 is a confirm-not-type. Consider making step 1 a single-tap continue when a name already exists. Also verify the onboarding CTA is a real, large, tappable button - the dead-click rate hints the primary action isn't registering taps on some mobile viewports.

3. /checklist and /gifts get quick-backed - expectation mismatch

What went wrong

37.5% of /checklist sessions and 26% of /gifts sessions quick-back (land then immediately return). Users arrive expecting one thing and leave without engaging - likely a slow first paint on in-app browsers or content that doesn't match the entry point (email/ad promise).

Evidence

Clarity QuickbackClick per-URL: checklist 12/32 sessions, gifts 6/19 sessions.

Suggested fix

Check /checklist mobile load time and above-the-fold content in the IG/FB in-app browser. If checklist is being linked from emails, confirm the landing content matches the email's promise. Add a Clarity funnel from email → checklist to confirm the drop.

4. Dashboard dead + rage clicks - non-interactive elements look clickable

What went wrong

The dashboard has 47 dead clicks across 31 sessions plus 3 rage clicks - the only real rage cluster on the site. Something on the dashboard looks like a button and isn't (stat cards, an image, a badge).

Suggested fix

Watch 2–3 dashboard recordings to find the exact element, then either make it interactive or remove its button-like affordance (cursor, shadow, hover). Low effort, removes visible frustration.
