# Image-2 Prompts — Nesty Site Hero Images (v2)

Generate each in your image-2 web app at **1536×1024 (16:9 landscape)**, **high quality**.

These prompts are written to produce **warm, branded campaign-style images** — photographs with Hebrew headlines burned into them, brand colors as background washes, and an emotional "this is where your nest begins" feel. Each one is a self-contained brief.

---

## Brand cheat-sheet (referenced in every prompt)

- **Vintage Lavender** `#86608e` — primary brand color (washes, accents, headline color)
- **Dusty Rose** `#f4acb7` — warm accent
- **Soft Rose Light** `#ffcad4` — soft background washes
- **Cream/Oatmeal** `#faf8fb` — base background
- **Sage / natural wood** — grounding tones

**Typography (Hebrew headlines inside images):** modern sans-serif, soft and slightly rounded, similar to Assistant Bold or Heebo. Always RTL. Headlines large, generous, calmly placed — not loud. Color: vintage lavender on light backgrounds, white on lavender backgrounds.

**Mood:** warm, intimate, "your cool older sister set this up for you" — not sterile lifestyle stock.

**On Hebrew rendering:** image-2 does Hebrew well at high-quality but verify each rendering. If a letter looks off, regenerate that single image. Keep headlines short — under 5–6 words.

## 9. `how-it-works.png` — איך זה עובד? (home-page hero)

**This image fully REPLACES the entire current section** (the existing header "פשוט ככה, בשלושה צעדים" + the 3 cards underneath). It must be self-contained: headline, sub-line, three numbered visual steps, and three Hebrew step titles — all rendered into one beautiful frame. No body paragraphs needed; the visuals + titles tell the story.

### The image prompt — RTL step-by-step flow, self-contained section

This image is a **standalone branded section** for the home page. It works as a step-by-step instructional graphic that users can read like a Hebrew sentence — **right to left** — while still feeling warm, branded, and editorial.

**Important visual rules for this image:**
- This is a **section replacement**, not just decoration. The image must contain the section headline at the top, the three numbered steps as a flow, and three short Hebrew step titles — everything self-contained.
- The flow goes **right → left** (Hebrew reading direction). Step 1 is on the **RIGHT** of the frame, Step 2 in the CENTER, Step 3 on the **LEFT**.
- The **numbers (1, 2, 3) are the visual anchors** — large, confident, vintage-lavender circles. They draw the eye first and are unmistakable.
- A clear **connecting flow** (a soft hand-drawn dotted arrow / light curve) runs from the right circle (1), through the center circle (2), to the left circle (3). Tiny chevron arrowheads emphasize the right-to-left direction.
- Each step has its own product vignette below the numbered circle, plus a short Hebrew title.
- Aspect ratio is **slightly wider than typical** — consider 16:9 but the image will be displayed at full section width on desktop.

```

Modern Israeli apartment setting. ## Quick QA after you generate

For each image, check:

- [ ] Hebrew letters are formed correctly (especially ב, כ, ם, ן, ץ, ף — final-form letters often go wrong)
- [ ] No accidental English fragments inside the image
- [ ] Vintage lavender (`#86608e`) appears as a wash or accent — not just any purple
- [ ] No people, faces, or hands snuck in
- [ ] 16:9 aspect, high resolution, no compression artifacts on text

If a single image looks off, regenerate **only that one** with the same prompt. Image-2 generates fresh each time — small variations between runs are normal.
